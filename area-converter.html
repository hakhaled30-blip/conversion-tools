About Area Conversion

Area conversion is used to change a measurement from one unit of surface area to another while keeping the same physical area.

This calculator makes it easy to convert between metric and imperial area units without manual calculations.

How to Use This Converter

Enter the area value you want to convert, choose the original unit, select the target unit, and click the Convert button.

You can also use the swap button to quickly reverse the conversion direction.

Supported Area Units

Square Meter (m²) — Standard metric area unit.
Square Kilometer (km²) — Used for large areas.
Square Centimeter (cm²) — Used for small surfaces.
Square Millimeter (mm²) — Used for very small areas.
Square Mile (mi²) — Imperial unit for large areas.
Square Yard (yd²) — Common imperial area unit.
Square Foot (ft²) — Commonly used for buildings and rooms.
Square Inch (in²) — Used for small imperial areas.
Acre (ac) — Commonly used for land measurement.
Hectare (ha) — Common metric unit for land areas.
