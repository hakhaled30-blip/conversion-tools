About Currency Conversion

Currency conversion allows you to convert an amount from one currency to another using current exchange rates.

Select the currency you want to convert from, choose the target currency, enter an amount and press Convert.

This tool supports major international currencies and is designed to work smoothly on phones, tablets and computers.
